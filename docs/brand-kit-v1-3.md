# I Wear Organic
## Brand Kit + Digital Style Guide
**Version 1.3**

Use this document as the source of truth for **iwearorganic.com**, future ecommerce experiences, and future iOS product work.

This system should guide:
- visual design
- typography
- color
- spacing
- UI components
- content hierarchy
- photography
- copy tone
- ecommerce behavior
- responsive implementation

Do not introduce visual or content patterns that conflict with this system unless explicitly approved.

---

# 1. Brand Foundation

## Brand Name
**I Wear Organic**

## Category
Organic apparel and lifestyle ecommerce.

## Core Positioning
I Wear Organic is a modern apparel brand where organic is not a special collection, filter, or seasonal category.

**Organic is the entire store.**

The experience should feel like a contemporary premium fashion brand first, with organic materials built naturally into the product proposition.

## Brand Promise
**If you wear it from I Wear Organic, it’s organic.**

## Primary Tagline
**Wear Better. Naturally.**

## Core Brand Statement
**Organic isn’t a collection. It’s the entire store.**

# 3. Brand Principles

## Organic First
Organic is not a special collection.

It is the standard across the store.

---

## Simple & Modern
Use clean layouts, minimal decoration, generous spacing, and timeless visual decisions.

---

## Fashion Forward
The first reaction should be:

**“I want to wear that.”**

The second reaction should be:

**“And it’s organic.”**

The brand should feel like fashion rather than an environmental campaign.

---

## Human & Approachable
Photography, language, and UI should feel warm, confident, inclusive, and natural.

---

## Editorial Imagery
Use:
- natural light
- real people
- texture
- movement
- everyday environments
- tactile material detail

---

## Consistent & Calm
Use generous space, refined details, limited visual noise, and a focused shopping experience.

# 6. Soft Minimal Descriptor

The phrase:

**SOFT MINIMAL**

appears within the brand-board presentation as a design-direction descriptor.

It is **not required as part of the primary public-facing logo**.

Do not automatically place it beneath the I Wear Organic wordmark throughout the website.

Use only in specifically approved brand lockups or applications.

---

# 7. Trademark Usage

Approved wordmark variants may include:

**i wear organic™**

or

**i wear organic®**

Use **® only when legally registered for the relevant goods/services.**

Trademark marks should:
- be superscript
- remain visually small
- sit at the upper-right of the wordmark
- maintain appropriate optical spacing

Do not enlarge or emphasize the trademark symbol.

# 11. Color Distribution

Target approximately:

- 60–70% White
- 15–20% Natural Cotton
- 5–10% Charcoal
- 5–10% Organic Forest
- <5% Sage, Clay, or Soil

The experience should remain bright, calm, and spacious.

Organic Forest should feel intentional rather than dominant.

# 16. Typography Rules

Use Sora consistently throughout the digital experience.

Avoid:
- unnecessary typeface mixing
- overly dramatic font-size jumps
- excessive uppercase copy
- extreme tracking
- decorative text effects
- outline text
- text shadows

Use uppercase sparingly for:
- small labels
- eyebrow copy
- navigation utility labels

Maintain generous line-height.
