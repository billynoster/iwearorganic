# 5. Wordmark Typeface

The logo direction is based on a **Core Sans-style contemporary sans treatment**.

The approved wordmark artwork should always take precedence over recreating the logo using live browser text.

### Important

Do not recreate the I Wear Organic logo using:

- Manrope
- Sora
- Inter
- Arial
- system fonts

Use the supplied SVG or approved vector artwork whenever possible.

Site implementation: `public/brand/logos/iwo-wordmark-*.svg` via `<Logo />`.

---

# 6. Wordmark Usage

Primary:

**i wear organic**

Optional trademark:

**i wear organic™**

Use lowercase only.

Primary color:

**Organic Forest**

`#294936`

---

# 7. Trademark

The `™` should:

- sit at the upper-right of the final letter
- be visually subordinate
- remain clearly legible
- use approximately 20–30% of the wordmark cap height
- align optically rather than mathematically

Do not allow the trademark symbol to dominate the logo.

# 8. Logo Descriptor

The phrase:

**SOFT MINIMAL**

was used during brand exploration to describe the selected direction.

It is **not part of the primary consumer-facing wordmark**.

Do not automatically include it below the logo on the website.

# 13. Primary Digital Typeface

# Manrope

Manrope is the primary typeface for the digital brand system.

Use for:

- headlines
- body copy
- navigation
- product cards
- buttons
- forms
- labels
- ecommerce UI
- app UI
- marketing copy

It provides a modern, refined visual language without becoming overly blocky or technical.

---

# 14. Manrope Weights

## Regular
`400`

Use for:

- body copy
- descriptions
- long-form content
- secondary UI

## Medium
`500`

Use for:

- navigation
- buttons
- product names
- labels
- supporting headings

## Semibold
`600`

Use selectively for:

- important headlines
- strong product statements

Avoid excessive use of Bold or ExtraBold.

---

# 15. Headline Direction

Primary brand headlines should generally use:

**Manrope Medium**

rather than very heavy weights.

The goal is quiet confidence.

Examples:

**Wear Better. Naturally.**

**Made for Every Day.**

**Naturally Your Own.**

**Organic Isn’t a Collection. It’s the Entire Store.**

---

# 16. Optional Editorial Typeface

## Neue Haas Grotesk

Neue Haas Grotesk may be introduced selectively for:

- premium campaigns
- editorial stories
- lookbooks
- seasonal art direction
- major fashion statements

It should not be required for core ecommerce functionality.

Use only where licensing and implementation allow.

If unavailable, use Manrope.

---

# 17. Alternative Editorial Typeface

## Instrument Sans

Instrument Sans may be used as an alternative contemporary editorial direction if a campaign requires slightly more personality.

Do not mix:

Core Sans + Manrope + Neue Haas + Instrument Sans

within a single standard ecommerce page.

The default system remains:

**Core Sans-style Wordmark + Manrope Digital UI**

# 19. Typography Rules

Do:

- use lighter visual weights
- allow whitespace around headlines
- maintain comfortable line-height
- keep text blocks concise
- favor sentence case

Avoid:

- overly heavy bold headlines
- oversized typography purely for visual impact
- excessive uppercase
- narrow line-height
- extreme tracking
- decorative text effects

# 47. Hoodie Drawstrings

For branded hoodie imagery:

Drawstrings should remain short.

Target:

approximately `4–5 inches`.

They must:

- stop above logo
- never touch the wordmark
- never cross the wordmark
- remain outside the print area
- remain symmetrical

- decorative 3D animation

# 58. Cursor Implementation Rules

When modifying I Wear Organic:

1. Use the approved SVG for the wordmark.
2. Do not recreate the logo with Manrope.
3. Treat the logo as its own Core Sans-style brand asset.
4. Use Manrope as the primary digital typeface.
5. Prefer Manrope Medium for headlines.
6. Prefer Manrope Regular for body copy.
7. Avoid heavy typography.
8. Remove Sora from the active design system.
9. Remove Inter from the primary design system.
10. Do not introduce serif typography without explicit approval.
11. Use Organic Forest intentionally.
12. Keep White and Natural Cotton dominant.
13. Maintain generous whitespace.
14. Keep product imagery dominant.
15. Keep homepage sections limited.
16. Prioritize ecommerce usability.
17. Avoid unsupported environmental claims.
18. Maintain accessibility.
19. Avoid generic eco branding.
20. Keep the experience fashion-first.
